| Verdict | Gap | First decision |

## What the checks actually observe

Baseline: `643ae7d5dea236e2b9fc324efd14ec3bdceea2a2` of main, read 2026-11-25. The weekly workflow repeats ordinary tests, workspace mutation and per-crate coverage. Mutation asks whether existing tests distinguish a changed program. Coverage records which regions run. Neither supplies a chess answer. [Workflow](../../.github/workflows/check.yml) · [Mutation command](../../scripts/check-mutants) · [Local check](../../scripts/check)

- **Rules.** Seeds 1–21 from the starting position and 1–4 from five published positions repeat. The test requires castling, promotion and an en passant capture. At each position it compares check, legal UCI moves and every resulting FEN. Only shakmaty continues the game. Rebuilding fisher from FEN can repair a stale internal value before its next use. [Rules comparison](../../crates/rules/tests/positions.rs)
- **Published answers.** Ordinary tests cover shallow perft and ten of 100 published mates. The selectable deeper rules test and four-ply search and reference node checks are ignored. The ignored mate test covers all 100, including the ten. Proposed weekly release run: all six rules positions through depth four, both four-ply node tests and all 100 mates. Record depths, revision and elapsed time. Higher published depths remain available on request. [Rules tests](../../crates/rules/tests/positions.rs) · [Search tests](../../crates/search/tests/search.rs) · [Reference tests](../../crates/search/tests/reference_minimax.rs) · [Published data](../../expectations/README.md)
- **Search.** Three fixed games supply twelve roots each at depth two. Separate examples reach depth four. The reference computes material minimax over shakmaty moves, including exact node counts. The variation checker replays moves and compares the score along the way. Once fisher-3k5 changes evaluation and the horizon, the old centipawn answers lose their authority. Even a score-preserving cutoff changes the work counted. Keep published mates, legal replay and a bench signature for behavior-preserving changes. Require an independently specified evaluation before demanding new exact scores, and SPRT for a strength claim. [Search comparison](../../crates/search/tests/search.rs) · [Reference](../../crates/search/tests/support/reference_minimax.rs) · [Variation checker](../../crates/search/tests/support/principal_variation.rs) · [Evidence](../evidence.md)
- **UCI.** Ten inputs through length four give 1 + 10 + 100 + 1,000 + 10,000 threaded histories. Named longer tests already cover queued commands and stop crossing search completion. The history checker has its own tests. Binary transcripts check real process output, and controlled schedules fix command order and elapsed time. A seed reproduces generated commands. The OS can deliver worker reports in a different order on replay. [Threaded histories](../../crates/uci/tests/threaded_session.rs) · [History rules](../../crates/uci/tests/support/search_history.rs) · [Checker tests](../../crates/uci/tests/search_history.rs) · [Binary transcripts](../../crates/fisher/tests/transcripts.rs) · [Timing expectations](../../expectations/README.md)
- **Coverage.** Bench has a failed position write test. Its final total write and flush cost another full bench each. Fixed perft input leaves invalid FEN uncovered there. Valid constructors exclude missing kings and out-of-bounds squares in rules. Deepening and UCI have gaps in inline tests. UCI also has formatting errors, reader exit after disconnect and OS thread-spawn failure. Successful token peek and checked slice length make two defensive branches unreachable. Both binary paths are exercised. Random legal games cannot force these failures. Retain the recorded explanations until their premises change. [Coverage notes](../guardrails.md)

## What to fund first

For every proposal below, an agent independent of the implementation writes the property, bounds and deliberate fault from the cited requirement before implementation. The owner approves them and reads the first failing example. The implementer writes the runner. Before any new CI time, require a clean run on the baseline and a failure on the approved broken copy. A timeout is inconclusive. Time limits below are proposed spending limits. Actual runtimes are unverified.

**Targeted generation** chooses inputs for a particular question. Shakmaty supplies legal moves and plays them independently. Keep both positions from the first FEN. Compare legal moves, check and full FEN after every selected move, including both clocks and castling rights. Reuse the published starts and existing dev dependency. Retain seed, revision, starting FEN and UCI moves for replay. First fault: lose castling rights on a quiet rook move from an unrelated square while the corresponding right still exists. Require replay to identify the first disagreement. Cap weekly generation at ten minutes and report games and special moves reached. Review a replay when it fails. Settle disagreements by the chess rules and a third implementation. This buys new histories before committing to a catalogue of generators. Shared mistakes still need published answers. [Shakmaty 0.30.3, packaged README](https://docs.rs/crate/shakmaty/0.30.3/source/README.md) · [Disagreement policy](../evidence.md)

For the weekly published suite, reuse its existing expected values. No generator or new dependency is needed. The independent author selects the six depths and verifies that changing a mate result or perft calculation fails against the unchanged data. Allow 30 minutes for the first release run. The owner reads mismatches and runtime before approving the schedule.

**Search pilot.** Spend one day and at most ten release minutes on 36 fresh depth-three roots. Keep the exact reference comparison while its assumptions hold. Independently replay every reported principal variation, including its first move and any reported mate. Pass only if all roots finish, all comparisons agree and replacing a later move by an illegal move is detected and replayable. Maintain one generator alongside the current comparison. The owner reads the broken variation and runtime. Drop exact comparisons separately as their assumptions expire.

**Property testing** generates inputs from a declared range and checks a statement for each. Use proptest strategies to vary readiness, reports and release commands around this history: accept position A, go infinite, hold its move, stop, accept B, go, finish. Choose A and B with different forced best moves. Check one release per accepted search and legal ownership in both positions against the existing protected history rules. This interaction exceeds the four-event enumeration. It tests a specific carryover fault without pretending to control native threads. One day, 256 scripted cases, two minutes per run. Pass only if all cases finish cleanly and retaining A's held move for B fails with a replayable history. Maintain the strategies when UCI changes. The owner reads the failing history beside the approved protocol rule. [Proptest 1.6.0](https://docs.rs/proptest/1.6.0/proptest/) · [History rules](../../crates/uci/tests/support/search_history.rs)

## What would change the decision

Each first experiment below gets one day locally and ten minutes per invocation. Add no CI job unless the baseline meets the stated property within the limit and the deliberate fault violates it. Keep the harness or model only while an independent reviewer can maintain its link to the production behavior. Tool sources were read on 2026-11-25. The cited versions identify the documentation examined.

| Tool and first target | Trigger and limit of the evidence | Setup, upkeep and owner read |
|---|---|---|
| **Loom** explores thread orderings using instrumented synchronization. Race stop against a worker finishing and require exactly one best move. Break release-once and replay the failing order. | A duplicate, missing response or hang depends on scheduling. Only modeled operations and explored orderings count. | Substitute Loom primitives in the actual release path, mock external I/O, bound the threads. Keep the mapping current. Read the schedule against the history rule. [Loom 0.7.2](https://docs.rs/loom/0.7.2/loom/) |
| **cargo-fuzz with libFuzzer** mutates inputs to reach new code. Feed FEN text to the parser. Require a result or documented rejection without panic. Break a length check. | A malformed FEN escapes existing parser examples. Coverage alone cannot establish correct acceptance or chess legality. | Build an instrumented target and retain a small corpus. Review the shortest reproducer and the independent parser contract. [cargo-fuzz 0.12.0](https://github.com/rust-fuzz/cargo-fuzz/blob/0.12.0/README.md) · [libFuzzer, LLVM 18.1.8](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/llvmorg-18.1.8/llvm/docs/LibFuzzer.rst) |
| **Kani** checks Rust assertions for symbolic inputs in a harness. Check square conversion against the independent file/rank formula on the entire admitted integer range. Break one boundary. | Square representation or indexing changes. Constructors already restrict inputs and unsafe Rust is forbidden. The harness assumptions and loop bounds limit the claim. | Pin Kani and maintain one small harness over production code. Read the assumed domain, completeness of bounds and counterexample. [Kani 0.56.0](https://github.com/model-checking/kani/blob/kani-0.56.0/README.md) · [Rust decision](../adr/0001-rust-with-unsafe-forbidden.md) |
| **TLA+** describes transitions. TLC checks a finite model against invariants. Model two outstanding searches and require each released move to belong to its accepted search. Break the association. | A protocol proposal permits overlapping searches. Today's history checker already states the single-search rules. A correct model can describe the wrong Rust behavior. | Pin TLC and Java, bound searches and messages, maintain an event-to-Rust mapping. Read the transitions and trace alongside a matching transcript. [TLC 1.7.4 command and checks](https://github.com/tlaplus/tlaplus/blob/v1.7.4/tlatools/org.lamport.tlatools/src/tlc2/TLC.java) |
| **Lean** checks formal proofs of stated theorems. Prove a cutoff preserves the root score on finite material minimax trees. Require a checked proof without assumed conclusions and a counterexample to a deliberately reversed bound. | Two search implementations disagree on a cutoff and the intended bound is disputed. A theorem about trees leaves Rust's implementation and evaluation to check separately. | Pin Lean, formalize minimax and maintain the correspondence to Rust. The owner reads the theorem, assumptions and correspondence with an independent proof reviewer. [Lean 4.19.0 and theorem-proving documentation](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/v4.19.0/README.md) |

For the remaining coverage gaps, prefer deterministic failure injection at the boundary that changes. A replacement bench writer should return the injected final flush error. A changed UCI reader should exit on disconnect. A new fallible thread factory should propagate a forced spawn failure. The independent author derives each expected error from the caller contract. Review one failing regression and its cost. For test-only or unreachable regions, reopen the coverage decision when the constructor, input data, token or slice precondition changes. No verifier needs to manufacture an impossible public input.
